# sega_sound.txt - the Mega Drive Dune II's sounds, as General Sound
# modules.
#
# tools/sega/sega_mod.py reads this and writes every line's module into
# src/res/prebuilt/music/ or src/res/prebuilt/sfx/, converted from what the
# cartridge's sound driver tells its chips - the song data, the
# instruments and the samples - never from a recording.
#
#   KIND   NAME              BANK   SONG
#
# KIND is music (instruments playing patterns: a tracker module that
# loops where the song does) or sfx (rendered whole through the chip model
# into one sample, played once).  BANK is game (installed at $02DCC8, 84
# songs) or front (installed at $02DD14 for the title, 13 songs).  Names
# are the options screen's own ($087F3C, $087FD4) where it has one.
