I took the comparison tool's own ranking and worked down it. The options
screen was the furthest from the demo at 1.39; it is 0.64 now, and two
traps came out of doing it.

The options screen

The demo puts one big ornithopter on a field of gold with two settings on
top. The port had a list of keys, white on black - more useful on a machine
with a different keyboard, and worth keeping, but there was no reason for
it to look nothing like the demo.

So: the aircraft's body is read out of the capture now - one line in
nes_art.txt, `image thopter at 0,152 size 256x88 from options` - and drawn
under the port's own words, on the same gold. The words moved up into the
clear part, as the demo has them.

Two traps, both worth writing down

A plane byte is two pixels, not a colour. I set the screen's clear colour
to gold and got vertical stripes. vid_clear writes the byte it is given
into both planes, so colour 7 paints 00000111 - one gold pixel and one
black - all the way across. I guessed wrong twice about the layout before
reading .claude/docs/graphics.md, which says it plainly: bits 0-2 and 6 are
the left pixel, bits 3-5 and 7 the right. vid_fill takes a colour and packs
it properly; it is in video.asm beside vid_clear and the docs now say when
each is right.

The front palette is chosen by area, so a picture can vote the words off
it. Adding the ornithopter dropped pure white from the sixteen - thirty
thousand gold pixels outrank a few hundred white ones - and every caption
in the front end went cream. choose_palette now pins white as well as
black for the front end.

The measurement was wrong too

The port's screenshot is the whole 768x576 frame, border and all, and the
border is black. So every scene was scoring partly on how much black border
it had, which flattered the battle in particular. compare.py crops the
320x200 screen out of the frame now, and the numbers moved:

  intro 0.215   title 0.257   options 0.638
  house 0.352   mentat 0.216  battle 0.488

The battle went 0.28 to 0.49 - it had been getting credit for its border.
That is the one to chase next: the port's sand is a more saturated orange
than the demo's and its rock is browner.
