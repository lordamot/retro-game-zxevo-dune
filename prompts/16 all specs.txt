- S6 production: build times about a quarter of the PC's; what the factories offer is hard-coded by campaign; production that runs out of money resumes by itself; the computer's build picks were replayed exactly from its random numbers.
- S7 the enemy: the script machine is the PC's, and a Python interpreter reproduced 33,926 script ticks exactly; computer teams never recruit, because the recruit call walks the enemy's list, so the computer fights only through its units' own orders.
