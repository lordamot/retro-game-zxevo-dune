All four are fixed and merged into the main tree, and nothing is committed yet. On the combined tree make build and make verify pass, and make test passes too: every suite, all 27 missions, worst window 1.11 frames a pass.

Harvester (the critical one). There were two causes.
It never came out: on the way home the Harvester and the Refinery claim each other through script variable 4. On docking, the cartridge's unit_drop_references ($04900E) breaks that claim both ways; the port's did not. So the Refinery's script found the link still there after unloading, skipped release and waited for ever.
It was not drawn on the pad: the cartridge shows the sprite of a docked Harvester while it is off the map (c_spr_show $048966), and the port drew no off-map unit at all. There is now a flag for that, set on docking and cleared when the unit is taken off, picked up or released.
A new dock test checks the whole cycle, and it fails on the old code. In Atreides 1 the Harvester now unloads on the pad, drives out, harvests and comes back.

Cursor. On the cartridge (ui_battle_button $028596) the shape depends only on what is selected, not on what is under the cursor. One of your units: the crosshair, and a white bracket on the unit blinking 11 frames on, 11 off. An enemy unit or your Saboteur: the square and a red bracket. A structure: blinking corners. An armed Palace: the crosshair. Before, the port always drew the square pointer, and a selected unit got a box of corners that did not blink. Checked against the running cartridge, with two new test suites.

Yard OK. The port had never drawn the cartridge's structure markers. They are now drawn as struct_animate ($00DC98-$00DDA2) sets them: OK on a Yard whose building waits and which is not selected (B deselects it, so OK appears), OK on a ready Palace, the repair hammer, and a Windtrap's low-power bolt.

Mentat's mouth. The port chose a new mouth every 4 frames. The cartridge (mentat_animate_face $01F622) holds each shape for a time that depends on the shape, about 12.8 frames a change, and the port now does the same (12.0). Two more timing differences were fixed in the same routine: the pause after a sentence is one and a half times as long as the sentence took, not a fixed 40 frames, and there is a wait before the first sentence.

Two things to know. First, S10 says the pointer's shape follows what is under it, and the cartridge says otherwise; I left the spec as it is. Second, the four worktrees and their branches are still there.
