What S1 found
- One tick is one frame at 50 Hz. The cartridge is PAL-only, which matches the ZX Evolution's 50 Hz, so tick counts carry over one to one.
- Each pass of the loop runs the units and then one of teams, structures, houses, structures, in turn. The PC game ran all four every pass. So structures run every 2nd pass and teams and houses every 4th; the structure script's 5 ticks really come out as 6.
- The clock runs only while a battle frame hook is installed and the selection mode is 1-4. The Start menu pauses the game by taking the hook out, which was measured.
- Timers are never reset between missions; the clock just keeps counting.
- Differences from the PC game: unit turning every 2 ticks, not 4; no blinking tick; a unit script budget of 49/17 against the PC's 52/3; Starport delivery counted every 60 ticks, not 180; turrets halve their script delay; decay is the house's amount plus one; the credit cap runs every house pass on whichever house the last loop visited; a Start/A/B press cuts the unit, house and team loops short; a Starport restock turns 0 into 1 where the PC skips it.
- Record details settled along the way: a house record is $46 bytes, not $26 (the names file header is corrected). $FFC148 is the PC's g_validateStrictIfZero. $FFC348 counts Starport purchases not yet delivered. Unit type +$0C bit 6 is hasTurret and bit 11 is scriptNoSlowdown; building type +$0C bit 1 is factory.
