The start-up is now two screens, and nothing loads while music plays.

Screen 1 is a black log with white text, left-aligned. Each line appears when its step runs, and "ok" or "error" is added when it finishes. An error stops the start-up for good (for example, with no card the log ends at "GS detect error").

  ZX Evo BaseConf detect ok
  GS detect ok
  GS memory 2Mb ok
  GS init done, loading
  Sound effects ok
  Music: intro ok
  Music: ordos dirge ok
  ... one line per tune ...
  Music: title ok

The progress bar sits along the bottom as before. The "GS memory" line shows up first and waits during the card's own memory test (about 11 s after power-on); the size is added when the test is done.

The checks are deliberately simple:
- ZX Evo BaseConf: the memory manager puts RAM pages 64, 128, 192 and 255 into a window, and each one has to keep its own byte. That proves 4 MB of separately addressable pages, which a smaller machine can't give. The bytes are put back afterwards.
- GS detect: the existing card probe.
- GS memory: after the card's test, command $23 gives its page count. On your line you wrote "4Mb", but the line shows the card's real size: 2Mb for this project's card. Fewer pages than the game needs gives an error.

Screen 2 is your Russian text with PRESS ANY KEY blinking under it while the intro tune plays. A key (keyboard or joystick) fades the screen out, and the Dune opening starts. The key is waited up again so the opening doesn't treat it as a skip.

About the strange sounds: I couldn't reproduce them. In the emulator the old version's loading under the intro had zero buffer underruns. The likely cause is still that loading: while the card plays a tune, a tune sent to it competes with its one Z80 for time. So I took it out entirely: every tune now goes to the card before any music starts, and nothing loads under the intro. A recording of the new start-up is flat silence until the intro tune starts. The one exception is a single level step at about 10 s, when the card's reset sets its outputs to mid-level. That comes from the card itself; if it's what you heard as a click, it happens once per power-on.

It's also faster: the log ends and the intro starts at about 29.5 s. Before, the screen was black until 22 s and loading went on under the intro until 37 s.
