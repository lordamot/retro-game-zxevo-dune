The game is now playable from power-on: the Mega Drive's front end leads into a battle, a structure's panel starts production, the building goes down from the Yard, and a won or lost mission goes on to the next one or back to the same one. make build and the full verify pass. Nothing is committed.

What verify checks now, all by pressing keys in the emulator:
- EGA at 14 MHz; Atreides 1 loads; A selects a vehicle, A again gives it Move and it drives off; the credits counter shows the house's credits.
- A on the Yard twice opens its build panel; the Windtrap is taken, built, and put down below the Yard.
- From power-on, A on every screen (logos, title, crests, mentat, briefing, campaign map) reaches Atreides 1; a lost mission goes through the Defeat, mentat and score screens and starts again.
- A battle pass takes 1.8 frames; the sound card loads and the battle music is audible.
- By hand as well: a won Atreides 2 goes through Victory, the score page and the password for mission 3 (SPICEDANCE) into the Atreides 3 briefing and battle.

Bugs found and fixed on the way
- No mission loaded: the loader called another bank with a near call, and its reset wiped the mission number after setting it; every house also got index 0.
- The side lists were never reset to "none", and linking a slot twice made it its own successor: Atreides 2 hung at its start, and destroying buildings could hang the target search.
- The renderer: a loop that never stopped at its end (a pass had taken 9 frames), a sprite entry one byte short, and a per-sprite dirty test that cost more than the drawing; the test is per square now, which roughly halves the renderer's cost on a full 64x64 mission.
- A debug handle came out of it: tests can call any routine inside a running battle (tools/dune_test.py Machine(..., battle=N)).
