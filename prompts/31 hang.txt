Committed: 4b044da (the start-up rework left from the previous session) and 0ddf11a (the disk delivery, the palette in the border, the boot's RAM clear, the Tutorial below #E0, the emulator's --sd and fillram, the libxpeccy card patch).

The hang. Your observation is the clue: the tune plays on, so the card's mixer is alive, and the game sits in one of its waits on the card. Every wait in src/gs.asm on the card's status port was unbounded - gs_wcmd (a command taken), gs_wdat (a data byte taken), and the inline loops in gs_block, gs_send, gs_send_delta, gs_fetch and gs_stream. A card that keeps mixing but never takes the next command freezes the Z80 for good, and the video shows exactly that screen: the region piece still on the map's dark screen, the level's first commands to the card never answered.

Two things built:

1. Every card wait is now bounded, about 4 seconds at 14 MHz (GS_STALL). When it runs out the card is given up - gs_ok = 0, gs_dead = 1 - the waiting routine carries on as if answered, and every later sound call does nothing. The game goes on in silence. So on the real machine a stuck card now shows as "the music stops after a few seconds and the level starts" instead of a hang, which is itself the diagnosis.

2. The test switch you asked for: debug flag 32 (DBGF_NOLOAD, src/world.asm bit 5). With it only the intro's tune goes to the card at start-up, nothing is loaded from the game's loops afterwards (gs_step does nothing, gs_intro_done does not refill the card), and a tune that is not on the card simply means silence. The code stays; the flag selects. The build is in build-noload/ (python3 tools/build_dune.py --build-dir build-noload --flags 32): build-noload/dune.trd and build-noload/DUNE.DAT.

What to try, in this order, and what each outcome means:
- build/dune.trd + build/DUNE.DAT (normal, with the bounded waits). If the level starts after a few seconds of silence, the card stopped answering a command and the card is the problem; if it starts with music, the earlier hang was a wait that a real card answers late and the timeout is now the whole fix.
- build-noload/dune.trd + build-noload/DUNE.DAT. If this one starts levels normally (silent), the background loading - the chunks written into the card's pages under the ROM's "do not mix" flag at $4086 while a tune plays - is what the card cannot take, which points at a ROM other than 1.05a or a card that reads its status differently.

Either way I need to know which card it is (General Sound revision and clock, or a NeoGS, and the ROM version), since the port writes into that ROM's variables by address; a different ROM explains a stuck command, the slowed tempo and the late start together.
