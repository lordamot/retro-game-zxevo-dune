GS 2Mb. The card's own logic source (UzixLS/zx-multisound, cpld/rtl/top.v) settles what the log can mean. Its 1 MB build ignores page bit 5, so pages 32-63 are pages 0-31 again - and the ROM's test catches exactly that and would say 31 pages, 1Mb. Its 2 MB build selects two more RAM chips for pages 32-63. Your card reported 62, and the check I added ran on it and found both of its marks intact in every one of the 62 pages - which chips that are not there cannot return. So the memory is there: either the board has two more chips on the back (the silk says "+1Mb"), or its logic maps the upper pages somewhere real. Either way the log is telling the truth, the tunes are not landing on each other, and nothing needs fixing. If you turn the card over and count the RAM chips, that closes it.

The hammer and the bolt. That was the port's. The cartridge's sprite engine gives every marker sprite a blink period - 3 for the bolt, 6 for OK, 7 for the hammer - and spr_render then skips it for one frame in period + 1: the bolt is off every fourth frame, OK every seventh, the hammer every eighth. The port flashed only OK, slowly, and drew the other two steady. All three now flash by the engine's rule, from the frame counter. The UI tests still pass.

The 4-slab above the Yard. I checked those four squares in the emulator: all four are mostly-rock and paveable, and the two top ones are two squares from the Yard. A 4-slab is placed by one check on the whole footprint, which passes because its bottom row touches the Yard - so the footprint is green - and then each square is paved by its own 1x1 check, and a 1x1 slab must touch the base: one of the squares round it must be your structure, concrete or wall. The top row has nothing round it yet, so it fails and the bottom row is paved. That is the cartridge's routine ($00E87E, "a 4-Slab paves what it can") and the PC rule it inherited: concrete grows outward from the base a row at a time, and the footprint is one sprite in one of two colours, not four squares each in its own. I have not changed it. If you want to see it on the cartridge itself, make run sega with DIPLOMATIC does the same.

The Light Factory's Quad. Also the cartridge's. What a factory offers first is struct_default_build_type $010D9E, and for a Light Factory that is the Quad, the Harvester once upgraded - whatever the mission's tech allows - and B on an idle factory starts its item again with no tech check ($028462). The panel's list comes from the tech table and is right to show the Trike only. So on the Mega Drive too, B on a fresh Light Factory in the second level builds a Quad. The upgrade levels themselves are tested against the running cartridge (spec S6, s6_levels) and the port's chains against the model (upgradable 120/120, buildable 90/90). I have left it as the cartridge has it; say if you would rather the port refuse what the list does not offer.
