The sound. The MultiSound's GS runs the same ROM at a higher clock; the mixing rate and the tempo come from the card's timer, not the clock, and the ROM's tunes rendered under 1.05b are identical to 1.05a, so the card does not mix differently. What fits "sounds not always, music distorted" is memory: tunes landing on the effects and on each other. My first check marked two places per page and passed on your card; if the upper pages land on the lower ones at another offset - a page bit wired to an address line below A15 - two marks miss it. Now every page is marked at eight places 4 KB apart, and after each page the ROM's own page table is compared with what it was, so a page that is the ROM's RAM under another number is caught too. Try this build and read me the GS line of the log: if it says 1Mb the pages were the problem and the music should be right; if it still says 2Mb the memory is real and I look elsewhere. And the back of the card: how many RAM chips?
